    Start it before you pull out. It keeps playing with the phone locked, through Bluetooth or CarPlay (it shows up in Now Playing), and the car's skip buttons jump between exercises.

    A soft chime marks each new exercise. After a one-bar count-in, it plays the notes for you to sing along with (the current note lights up), moving up a half step each time without breaking the beat. Read through the exercises before you go.
